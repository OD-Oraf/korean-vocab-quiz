## 📚 New Vocabulary Lists

### 🍽️ Restaurant-Focused Lists (5 lists - 235 items total)

#### 1. **restaurant-basics.json** (38 items)
**Focus:** Essential restaurant interactions
- Greetings and politeness (안녕하세요, 감사합니다, 잘 먹겠습니다)
- Making reservations (예약했어요, 자리 있나요?)
- Basic ordering (주문할게요, 메뉴판 주세요)
- Payment phrases (계산해 주세요, 카드 돼요?)
- Takeout and delivery (포장해 주세요, 배달 돼요?)

**Example Phrases:**
- 메뉴판 주세요 - "Please give me the menu"
- 두 명이에요 - "Two people"
- 계산할게요 - "I would like to pay"

#### 2. **restaurant-ordering.json** (44 items)
**Focus:** Detailed ordering and special requests
- Pointing and ordering (이거 주세요, 저거 주세요)
- Portion sizes (2인분 주세요, 3인분 주세요)
- Asking for recommendations (추천 메뉴가 뭐예요?, 뭐가 맛있어요?)
- Spiciness adjustments (안 맵게 해주세요, 매운 맛 좀 덜어주세요)
- Modifications (빼주세요, 추가해 주세요)
- Order issues (이거 안 시켰는데요, 아직 안 나왔어요)

**Example Phrases:**
- 맵지 않게 해주세요 - "Please make it not spicy"
- 양파 빼주세요 - "Please remove the onions"
- 리필 돼요? - "Can I get a refill?"

#### 4. **restaurant-grammar.json** (48 items)
**Focus:** Essential grammar patterns for restaurant situations
- Request patterns (주세요, 해주세요)
- Intent declaration (~할게요)
- Polite offers (~하시겠어요?)
- Negative modifications (~지 않게)
- Polite tense endings (~어요/아요, ~았어요/었어요)
- Honorific imperatives (~으세요/세요)
- Question patterns (~이/가 뭐예요?, ~돼요?, ~있어요?)
- Politeness softener (좀)

**Example Patterns:**
- 주세요 - "Please give me (polite request)"
- ~할게요 - "I will~ (declarative intent)"
- ~하시겠어요? - "Would you like to~? (polite offer)"
- 좀 - "A little / please (softener)"

**Grammar Explanations Included:**
- How to use 아/어 주세요 for requests
- When to use ~지 않게 for negative modifications
- Understanding honorific forms like 드시겠어요?

- Leftovers (남은 거 싸주세요)

**Example Requests:**

- 화장실 어디 있어요? - "Where is the restroom?"
- 와이파이 비밀번호가 뭐예요? - "What's the wifi password?"

## 🎯 Key Features

### Real-World Phrases
All lists include **complete phrases**, not just isolated words:
- ❌ Just "spicy"
- ✅ "안 맵게 해주세요" (Please make it not spicy)

### Grammar Pattern Integration
The **restaurant-grammar.json** list teaches the building blocks:
- Request patterns (주세요, 해주세요)
- Polite endings (~요, ~세요)
- Question forms
- Softeners and politeness markers

### Practical Examples
Each item is something you'd actually say in a real situation:
- Ordering: "이거 주세요" (Please give me this)
- Customizing: "매운 맛 좀 덜어주세요" (Please reduce the spiciness)
- Asking: "추천 메뉴가 뭐예요?" (What do you recommend?)

## 💡 Usage Tips

### For Quiz Practice:
- Start with **Restaurant Basics** for foundational phrases
- Move to **Restaurant Grammar** to understand the patterns
- Practice **Restaurant Ordering** and **Food** together for real scenarios

### For Real-World Use:
- Combine grammar patterns with vocabulary
- Example: "물" (water) + "좀 주세요" (please give) = "물 좀 주세요" (Could I have some water please)
- Practice variations: "냅킨 좀 주세요", "젓가락 좀 주세요"
